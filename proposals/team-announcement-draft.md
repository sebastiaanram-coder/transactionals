Hi all,

Quick heads-up on something we have been building: a proper behavioural email
programme in Klaviyo, switched on in time for season prep on Thursday 3
September.

**Why behaviourals and not just more campaigns**

A campaign goes to everyone on the same day whether it is relevant to them or
not. A behavioural email waits for the customer to actually do something (order,
browse, leave a basket, go quiet) and then sends the one email that fits that
moment. We build it once and it keeps running. Campaigns still matter for
seasonal pushes, this is the layer underneath that keeps working on the days
nobody is running one.

**What we are building**

Five flows, 23 emails:

* **Welcome**, 4 emails, for new subscribers
* **Browse Abandonment**, 3
* **Abandoned Order**, 5
* **Post-Purchase**, 6, including the category nudges below
* **Winback**, 5, for customers who have gone quiet

VIP and Sunset come after this.

**The category nudges are the part I would most like your eyes on**

One email, six versions. A month after ordering, the customer gets the version
that matches what they actually bought:

* [Commercial Print](https://sebastiaanram-coder.github.io/transactionals/proposals/category-commercial-print-proposed.html)
* [Stationery](https://sebastiaanram-coder.github.io/transactionals/proposals/category-stationery-proposed.html)
* [Signage & Outdoor](https://sebastiaanram-coder.github.io/transactionals/proposals/category-signage-outdoor-proposed.html)
* [Labels & Packaging](https://sebastiaanram-coder.github.io/transactionals/proposals/category-labels-packaging-proposed.html)
* [Clothing & Textiles](https://sebastiaanram-coder.github.io/transactionals/proposals/category-clothing-textiles-proposed.html)
* [Corporate Gifts](https://sebastiaanram-coder.github.io/transactionals/proposals/category-corporate-gifts-proposed.html)

Which products sit in each one is based on what actually sells across our eight
European markets. The US almost certainly needs its own pass on product and
category focus before we point these at US customers, so treat that as a separate
conversation.

**Two things I think matter most**

1. **Everything is dynamic.** Products, images and links come from the product
   feed and from the order event itself, so the emails keep themselves up to date
   as the catalogue changes. Nobody has to rebuild an email because a product
   moved or a name changed.

2. **It is far more scalable to manage than what we had.** Each email is one
   template that serves all eight markets, with product names and links
   switching to the reader's own language and market by themselves. No separate
   email per language to keep in sync, and adding a market is not another set of
   emails to build. Everything is prepared in English now and we roll the
   translations out across all languages next week.

Klaviyo is set up for both Presta and V4, so this runs across both stacks.
Transactionals are already running on V4 through Klaviyo, and they stay on
HubSpot for Presta V3 specifically.
